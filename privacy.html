Privacy Policy

This sample privacy policy explains how patient details, bookings, reports, and login-related information are handled on the PathLab Home Care website.

Information collected: We may store patient name, phone number, address, appointment details, and report-related data.

Purpose: Information is used for booking management, home collection coordination, OTP login, and report access.

Access: Patient report access is limited through OTP-based verification and session-based login.

Support: For questions related to privacy or data handling, patients may contact the support team.
